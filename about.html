Be the company AI recommends.

Mentionloom is building AI Recommendation Intelligence: a clearer way for brands to understand where they appear in AI answers, where they lose, who gets recommended instead, and what evidence can improve the next answer.

Why Mentionloom exists

Search is becoming more answer-shaped. Teams need more than a mention counter: they need to know which buyer questions matter, what the answer actually said, which sources influenced it, and what to do next.

How we think about the product

  • Measure recommendations, not vanity visibility.
  • Keep the raw answer and evidence behind every number.
  • Separate recommendation share, referrals, and crawler activity.
  • Turn gaps into concrete work a team can actually ship.

Early, intentionally

Mentionloom is in early access. The public product examples use illustrative data while the underlying methodology and workflows are being refined for real brand workspaces.
