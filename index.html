What does Mentionloom measure?

The product is designed around three separate signals: mentions and citations in a defined set of AI answers, identifiable AI referrals to your website, and crawler requests in connected server logs. Each signal answers a different question; a bot visit alone does not mean your brand was recommended.

Can you see what people ask ChatGPT?

We cannot read people’s private conversations. You choose the buyer questions to monitor. Mentionloom is being built to run those questions through supported answer sources and compare the results over time. They are monitored prompts, not a feed of actual users’ searches.

Is this real customer data?

Acme is fictional. Provider and competitor logos do not imply a partnership. Early-access availability and collection coverage will be confirmed with your invitation.

Do I need to install a script?

The planned answer-monitoring workflow starts with your website and chosen questions. Website referral and conversion reporting needs a connected analytics source or tracking integration. Crawler reporting requires server or CDN logs. None of these can reveal every AI recommendation or unattributed visit.

Will this replace SEO or guarantee recommendations?

It complements your SEO work. The goal is to show which questions and sources deserve attention, so you can improve useful, accurate content. AI answers vary by engine, time, and context. No tool can guarantee that a model will recommend your company.

What happens after I join?

You’ll get an email when early access is available. Joining is free, with no payment details or commitment. Your confirmation includes a private link to manage or remove your signup.
