The details behind the promise.

What can Mentionloom actually see?

The product is designed to combine identifiable crawler requests, a defined sample of AI answers, and identifiable AI referral traffic. These are separate signals. We cannot see private AI conversations or every recommendation people receive.

Will this replace our SEO work?

No. Accurate, useful content and accessible pages still matter. Mentionloom helps you prioritize that work with evidence from AI discovery and follow up after you make a change.

Can we advertise in ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT offers advertising through official channels. Campaign support is a future direction for Mentionloom, separate from organic visibility. Anthropic currently says Claude will remain ad-free. Our pilot focuses on organic discovery.

Is the dashboard showing real customer data?

No. This is an interactive product preview with illustrative data and sample answers. Actual coverage will depend on the data sources you connect and the collection methods supported.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We save your email and invite you as access opens. You can optionally tell us about your website and priorities to help shape the product. Joining is free and does not commit you to a paid plan. Your confirmation includes a private signup link you can save to remove your details later.
